ratata curated and operated the official NFT booth ("NFT Positions") at Positions Berlin Art Fair 2022 during Berlin Art Week, held at Flughafen Tempelhof, Hangar 5–6. It was the fair's first dedicated NFT section, realised by Galerie Greulich and ratata with Samsung as technology partner.

The on-chain works were shown on thirteen Samsung The Frame displays, whose matte screens made many visitors look twice to tell a digital work from a painting or photograph. The centerpiece was a 110-inch Samsung The Wall provided for the section: with almost 2.8 meters of screen diagonal and a pixel pitch of just 1.2 millimeters, it rendered the finest details from nearly any distance and drew visitors to the NFT section from across the hangar.

The NFTs were minted on a climate-friendly proof-of-stake blockchain and consumed comparatively little energy per transaction. In cooperation with Below2, the remaining CO2 footprint of each NFT sale, including roughly one year of screen time, was offset.
