ratata operated the official NFT booth ("NFT Positions") at Positions Berlin Art Fair 2022 during Berlin Art Week, held at Flughafen Tempelhof, Hangar 5–6. Powered by Galerie Greulich and ratata, with Samsung as cooperation partner, the booth presented on-chain art on Samsung The Frame displays alongside the traditional gallery program.
