ratata operated its own booth throughout NFT Show Europe at the City of Arts and Sciences in Valencia on 14 and 15 July 2023. Phil Coufal and Johannes Simon represented the team across both days.

Commissioned by NFT Show Europe, ratata developed *rabbitcodes*, a wallet-based tool for ticket discounts. Token holders connected their wallet; the system checked for an eligible token and issued the corresponding discount code.
