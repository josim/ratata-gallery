"Collective Voice ID" is an interactive AI audio-visual installation by Portrait XO, with technical direction by ratata. It was presented at Zerospace, New York, on April 13, 2023, as part of Refraction Festival's NFT NYC exhibition, as an installation and performance including curation of the Sound Obsessed lineup, and again at the Istanbul Digital Art Festival from May 2–5, 2024.

The installation turns a visitor's voice into an animated, generative audio-visual "sonic portrait": the MFA Note Composer drives the generative audio, Dubler by Vochlea detects vowels through its AI-calibrated microphone, and Ebosuite maps those vowels to the visuals in real time. The workflow, a first for the artist, let visitors live-mint a few seconds of their own portrait as a sonified digital collectible: a reminder to celebrate their most unique sonic identity, the voice.
