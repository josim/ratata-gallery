*Collective Voice ID* is an interactive audio-visual installation and series by artist and researcher Portrait XO. The work translates participants’ voices into animated sonic portraits. ratata developed the technical infrastructure that allowed audiences to capture and mint these live outputs.

Portrait XO’s live system generated the sound and visuals and sent its output to ratata’s streaming infrastructure. A web interface connected each participant’s wallet to a dedicated smart contract. When a mint was requested, the system captured either a still image or the final ten seconds of the live stream, depending on the iteration, and minted the result on Tezos. Each presentation used its own contract because the output format evolved.

### VCA Invites London · 2022

The first documented presentation took place at VCA Invites London on 2 November 2022. Portrait XO presented *Collective Voice ID* through a panel, installation and performance, with the live-minting infrastructure provided by ratata.

### NFT NYC · 2023

On 13 April 2023, Portrait XO presented the installation and a performance at Zerospace in New York as part of Refraction Festival’s NFT NYC programme. A panel on *Merging Music, Art & Blockchain* followed on 14 April.

### Istanbul Digital Art Festival · 2024

The installation was shown at the Istanbul Digital Art Festival from 2 to 5 May 2024. For this iteration, live voices were combined with records of Turkey’s temperature anomalies from 1940 to 2024, connecting each generated portrait to a wider history of environmental change.
