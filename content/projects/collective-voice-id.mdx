"Collective Voice ID" is an AI audiovisual installation with technical direction by ratata. It was presented at NFT NYC at Zerospace, New York, on April 13, 2023, as an installation and performance including curation of the Sound Obsessed lineup, and again at the Istanbul Digital Art Festival from May 2–5, 2024.
