From 18 to 20 November 2022, the pop-up market *Fashion/Design: Made in Frankfurt* brought together 40 local creatives and designers at Bethmannhof, alongside catwalks, live music and DJ sets.

For the three-day event, ratata and Galerie Greulich jointly curated and presented a digital art exhibition featuring works by Hoxid, AdiOohgaga, Rose Jackson and Marissa Noana. The presentation remained on view throughout the market. The identified works are documented below.
