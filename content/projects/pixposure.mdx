*Pixposure* was a generative NFT art show curated and organised by ratata, presented at Galerie Greulich, Fahrgasse 22, Frankfurt am Main. The exhibition ran from 9 January to 13 February 2022.

Alongside the show, the gallery hosted an accompanying program: a talk on generative art, a live minting session ("How to mint") with guests in the gallery, and an interview with Ivona Tau.

The exhibited works are documented below. Iskra Velitchkova's "insides" was shown unminted. msoriaro's "mechanisms: machine dreams xvi", a generative work shown as a looping video, is part of a collection by Marcelo Soria-Rodríguez documented at [iillucid.com](https://www.iillucid.com/art-by-marcelo).
