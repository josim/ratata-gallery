"Pixposure" was a generative NFT art show presented at Galerie Greulich, Fahrgasse 22, Frankfurt am Main, curated and organized by ratata. The exhibition ran from January 9 to February 13, 2022.

### Exhibited works

Ivona Tau - [Consciousness Horizon: Winter Night](https://objkt.com/tokens/KT1MAGZVMuLMu4SXVVxvTMdWL1ZXiqe8Hsaj/0)

Iskra Velitchkova - insides (not minted)

Sam Tsao - [Imaginary Resonance - Dithered](https://objkt.com/tokens/hicetnunc/634142)

shardcore - [Three Muses (2019)](https://objkt.com/tokens/hicetnunc/645534) and [Unchecked Desires (2019)](https://objkt.com/tokens/hicetnunc/645525)

msoriaro - [mechanisms: machine dreams xvi](https://objkt.com/tokens/hicetnunc/171342), a generative work by Marcelo Soria-Rodríguez shown as a looping video; more about [the collection](https://www.iillucid.com/art-by-marcelo)

Krankarta - [Drumlin #27](https://objkt.com/tokens/hicetnunc/619252)

Loackme - [the Fall](https://objkt.com/tokens/hicetnunc/631491)
