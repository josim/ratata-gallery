AI Hub Frankfurt Rhein-Main is an interdisciplinary network connecting research, culture, industry, economy, and politics under the sign of artificial intelligence. Its annual UAI Talent Night brings the network's members and the broader public together: in talks and panel discussions, experts from research and industry, alongside speakers from the Bundestag, Google, Microsoft, Deutsche Bank, and Commerzbank, share their experiences, insights, and ideas with the audience.

Culture had its own stage at the 2023 edition, held at Kunstverein Familie Montez: the AI Vernissage. ratata gallery was invited to present its AI art and experiments: explaining how the tools work, demonstrating them live, and showing what AI can do for artists. The vernissage featured AI artists Vladimir Alexeev (Merzmensch) and ratata creative director Adi Zufall.
