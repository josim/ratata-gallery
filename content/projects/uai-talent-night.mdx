More than 400 guests attended the UAI Talent Night: mAI Edition at Kunstverein Familie Montez in Frankfurt on 16 May 2023. Organised by AI Hub Frankfurt, the event brought together people from AI research, business, politics and culture for talks, discussions and an evening programme.

As part of the programme, the AI Vernissage presented a selection of AI-based works by Adi Phil Coufal (AdiOohgaga) of ratata and Vladimir Alexeev (Merzmensch). The works were shown across a series of screens in the event space; the presentation included AdiOohgaga’s *MEMOREVERSE*.
