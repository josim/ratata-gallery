*I, AIrtist – Art in the Age of AI* brought together eight international artists working with artificial intelligence across moving image, digital poetry and still imagery. Curated and produced by ratata with Galerie Greulich as partner, the exhibition was held at The Mixer in Frankfurt from 27 January to 25 February 2023.

Works by Din Burns, Ivona Tau, Linda Dounia, MCHX, Merzmensch, Memo Akten, Sasha Stiles and Shardcore were presented throughout the exhibition. The selection included both blockchain editions and unminted works; whether a work had been minted was not a criterion for inclusion.
