"I, AIrtist — Kunst im Zeitalter von KI" (Art in the Age of AI) was a group show exploring artistic practice with artificial intelligence, presented at The Mixer, Fahrgasse 22, Frankfurt am Main, in collaboration with Galerie Greulich. Curated and organized by ratata, the exhibition brought together eight international AI artists and ran from January 27 to February 25, 2023.
