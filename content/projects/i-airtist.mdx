"I, AIrtist" was an AI-art group show featuring Merzmensch, Sasha Stiles, and other artists, presented at Galerie Der Mixer in collaboration with Galerie Greulich, Frankfurt. Curation and organization were carried out by ratata, with the exhibition running from January 27 to February 25, 2023.
