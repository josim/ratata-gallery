For UAI, The AI Startup Event for Hesse, ratata curated and produced a one-day presentation of generative and AI art by AdiOohgaga and Ivona Tau. The works were on view at the Museum für Kommunikation in Frankfurt throughout the event on 16 May 2022. In a talk held in German, AdiOohgaga and Johannes Simon discussed generative art and the use of AI in artistic practice.
