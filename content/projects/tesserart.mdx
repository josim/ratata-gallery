Tesserart is an art portal and event-services platform founded in 2024. It provides infrastructure for publishing and minting digital art across websites, social channels and physical exhibitions, with a focus on live formats using Tezos.

ratata developed technical infrastructure and provided support for Tesserart’s early platform and event projects. The collaboration began with *Emotion Traces* by Vic Doval at theNoox Festival in Valencia on 21 and 22 February 2024. The platform launched in April, followed by Portrait XO’s *Collective Voice ID* at Istanbul Digital Art Festival in May and *The Frame* at ArtMeta Basel and TezDev Brussels.

### Tezos Realm at TOKEN2049

On 18 September 2024, Tesserart joined the Culture & Community Realm at Tezos Realm in CHIJMES Hall, Singapore, during TOKEN2049 week. The presentation featured *Formica*, a generative work by Alvis Lee, which visitors could mint live on Tezos. ratata provided technical support for Tesserart’s participation.
