At NFC Summit Lisbon, held at Pavilhão Carlos Lopes from 4 to 6 June 2025, ratata worked on two distinct projects: the physical and technical delivery of XCOLLABZ’s exhibition container and the smart-contract infrastructure for the collaborative NFT *NFTs Are Not Dead*.

### Echoes of Light

*Echoes of Light* was an immersive container exhibition curated by XCOLLABZ. ratata helped construct the installation, lining the interior walls with projection fabric and mounting three projectors from the ceiling. We also installed two works on the exterior and provided technical support throughout the three-day event. Inside, works by 25 artists played as a continuous projection; the two exterior murals were by OMGiDRAWEDit and Sutu.

### NFTs Are Not Dead

Commissioned by NFC Summit and curated by Grida, *NFTs Are Not Dead* brought contributions from 29 artists into a single group collage. ratata was not involved in its visual composition. We developed the smart contract and its on-chain revenue distribution, then handled the minting through our InfiniteInk platform.

Artists: Agoria, Aleksandra Art, BÈFÈ, Ben Skaar, Benjamin Bardou, Bosul Kim, Botto, Bryan Brinkman, Claire Silver, DeeKay, Eko33, Emily Xie, FvckRender, Hackatao, Ivona Tau, Kevin Abosch, Kika Nicolela, Laurence Fuller, Mad Dog Jones, Mr.Richi, OONA, OSF, Osinachi, Patrick Amadon, Sasha Stiles, ThankYouX, Trevor Jones, YUYU, and Zancan.
