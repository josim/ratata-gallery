ratata served as technical lead for two drops presented with Art on Tezos at NFC Summit (Non-Fungible Conference) Lisbon, June 4 to 6, 2025.

### Echoes of Light

"Echoes of Light" was an immersive container exhibition curated by XCOLLABZ: a modified shipping container with exterior murals by OMGiDRAWEDit and Sutu, and continuous projections by 25 artists running inside. The curation explored how light transforms environments and shapes our perception, with participating artists including goldcat, HOXID, Marcelo Pinel, Morpho, Jakestudyos, and Somfay.

### Legend Collab Artists

"NFTs are not dead" is the commemorative NFC 2025 x Tezos collage drop: a chaotic group collage featuring signature works by legendary artists of the space, directed by NFC, curated by Grida, and released in an edition of 248 through ratata's InfiniteInk platform.

Artists: Agoria, Aleksandra Art, BÈFÈ, Ben Skaar, Benjamin Bardou, Bosul Kim, Botto, Bryan Brinkman, Claire Silver, DeeKay, Eko33, Emily Xie, FvckRender, Hackatao, Ivona Tau, Kevin Abosch, Kika Nicolela, Laurence Fuller, Mad Dog Jones, Mr.Richi, OONA, OSF, Osinachi, Patrick Amadon, Sasha Stiles, ThankYouX, Trevor Jones, YUYU, and Zancan.
