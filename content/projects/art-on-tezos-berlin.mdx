Art on Tezos: Berlin was a three-day digital art event across five locations in Berlin, presenting more than 200 artists through 20 exhibitors: galleries, platforms, and curatorial collectives from the Tezos ecosystem. ratata took part with "Spark", an exhibition presented by InfiniteInk × The Lighthouse, which showed "Lost Machine Manual" by Kerim Safa alongside further works.
