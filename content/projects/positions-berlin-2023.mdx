ratata returned with the official NFT booth ("NFT Positions") at Positions Berlin Art Fair 2023 during Berlin Art Week, held at Flughafen Tempelhof, Hangar 5–6. The booth was realised by ratata and Galerie Greulich, with Vitra, Samsung, and Buchhandlung Walther König as cooperation partners.

The 2023 edition marked the fair's tenth anniversary and drew more than 28,000 visitors, among them over 4,000 invited collectors. At Hangar 6, Stand S01, the NFT section presented ten international artists on ten screens.

The section met with strong interest: digital art and NFTs have long since moved beyond a niche audience and are now discussed in earnest by longtime collectors as well. Vitra's stool-tools invited visitors to sit down and spend time with the works, and conversations at the booth were correspondingly in depth – most often around the question of how digital works can be presented at home or in an exhibition.
