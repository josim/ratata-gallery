At SCOPE22, the DSV Group’s business festival for the Sparkassen-Finanzgruppe, ratata and Galerie Greulich presented a joint booth at the Rheingoldhalle in Mainz on 20 September 2022. Invited by Frankfurter Sparkasse, ratata curated, installed and delivered a presentation of digital works by Ivona Tau, Ruben Fro, Burka Bayram, Marissa Noana and Sebastian Pfeifer.

The selection comprised Ivona Tau’s *The Time*, Ruben Fro’s *ATLANTIS - Drowning in Thoughts #2*, Burka Bayram’s *Odysseus VS Polyphemus*, Marissa Noana’s *skincare routine* and Sebastian Pfeifer’s *Trust Fall*. In a TED-style talk held in German, gallerist Andreas Greulich explained how NFTs work and what they offer artists.

S-Payment commissioned *Trust Fall* as an edition of 500 for Sparkassen employees. ratata developed the smart contract, supported Sebastian Pfeifer with minting and distributed one token to each of 500 wallets in a batch process. The wallets were assigned through the recipients’ email addresses.
