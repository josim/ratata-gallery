At Tezos Realm during TOKEN2049 week, Tesserart presented *Formica*, a generative work by new-media artist Alvis Lee. The event took place at CHIJMES Hall in Singapore on 18 September 2024.

Tesserart joined the Culture & Community Realm alongside projects including Objkt and akaSwap. Visitors could view *Formica* on site and mint the work live on Tezos. ratata provided technical support for the presentation and its live-minting infrastructure.
