InfiniteInk is a Tezos minting platform created and operated by ratata. It enables artists to build and deploy their own smart contracts and mint directly from their wallets. Contracts remain under the artists’ control and support one-of-ones, limited editions, open editions, and auctions. Published works remain accessible through established marketplaces such as objkt.

### Slippery When Bullish · OONA × OMGiDRAWEDit

InfiniteInk launched on 31 March 2025 with *Slippery When Bullish*, a collaboration between OONA and OMGiDRAWEDit described by the artists as “an announcement of a prophecy”. The work was released as a 48-hour open edition priced at 9.99 ꜩ. A total of 150 editions were minted.

### Blind Minting · OONA

On 1 May 2025, InfiniteInk introduced blind minting with OONA’s *Market Heat Maps — Treachery of Tickers*. The feature allows an artist to assign several possible assets to a release and define its edition size. Collectors mint without knowing which asset they will receive. The associated collection contains 14 tokens.

### Art on Tezos: Berlin · Lost Machine Manual

From 6 to 9 November 2025, InfiniteInk and The Lighthouse presented *Spark* as part of Art on Tezos: Berlin. The exhibition included *Lost Machine Manual* by Kerim Safa, a generative work developed with ratata as concept partner and technical lead. Its digital interface connected the generated works with an on-site thermal printer, translating each mint into a physical print designed to fade over time.
