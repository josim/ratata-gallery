InfiniteInk is a platform for artists to build, deploy, and fully own their custom smart contracts on Tezos, built and operated by ratata. Artists mint directly from their wallets to contracts they own, as one-of-ones, limited editions, open editions, or auctions, while their work stays visible on established marketplaces like objkt.
