*Lost Machine Manual* is a generative artwork by Kerim Safa that simulates a forgotten technical document. Its system produces unique pages combining procedural diagrams, schematic grids, and pseudo-engineering notes. Functional language gives way to subtle errors and instructions that suggest emotional or moral decay, bringing machine logic, bureaucracy, and entropy into the same visual system.

The work exists digitally and as thermal prints. Their heat-sensitive images gradually fade, making technological impermanence part of the work itself. ratata co-developed the concept and was responsible for the technical development and production. *Lost Machine Manual* was created for *Spark*, presented by InfiniteInk × The Lighthouse as part of Art on Tezos: Berlin.
