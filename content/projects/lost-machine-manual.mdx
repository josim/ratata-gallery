"Lost Machine Manual" is a generative artwork by Kerim Safa that simulates a forgotten technical document: the system produces unique pages of procedural diagrams, schematic grids, and pseudo-engineering notes, existing digitally and as thermal prints that slowly fade over time. ratata was tech lead and supported the development of the project. It was created for the "Spark" exhibition by InfiniteInk × The Lighthouse and shown at Art on Tezos: Berlin 2025.
