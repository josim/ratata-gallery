*Emotion Traces* by Vic Doval marked Tesserart’s first live-mint project. The animated GIF was presented at theNoox Festival at Veles e Vents in Valencia on 21 and 22 February 2024.

The same free open edition was available through three channels: a Farcaster Frame, Tesserart’s website and the exhibition itself. Across these routes, 4,107 editions were minted on Tezos.

ratata developed the technical infrastructure for the project. Tesserart published the edition, with Ibon Escalada Ruiz de Loizaga representing the platform on site.
