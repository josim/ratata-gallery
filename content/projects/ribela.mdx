Ribela Love Nature brought music and digital art into an open-air setting in Sarria, Galicia, on 26 and 27 July 2024. Curated by Pole, the art programme presented more than 100 works by over 30 artists across a large LED stage screen and projections distributed throughout the festival grounds.

Commissioned by Pole, ratata provided the technical direction for the art presentation. We set up circular projection surfaces and projectors, installed programmable LEDs in the windows of a stone building and around the bar, and coordinated the display systems across the site. When the festival’s video technician became unavailable, we also took over control of the main LED screen and ran it through to the end of the event.
