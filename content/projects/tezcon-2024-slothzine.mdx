*Slothzine* is a monthly web3 comic zine curated by Burka Bayram and Ozan Bilaloğlu. Each issue brings together comic artists whose work approaches leisure, creativity and the art of slowing down from a different angle.

For TezCon Seattle, the issues published up to that point were assembled into a continuous film lasting approximately four hours. The work played at the teia.cafe booth as part of an event spanning 27 screens, live music and conversations about projects being built in the Tezos ecosystem.

The film remains available as a long-form record of the 2024 presentation. Slothzine has continued to publish since the event; the complete current series of 50 issues is documented below.
