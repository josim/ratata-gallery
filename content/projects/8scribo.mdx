Launched by ratata on 17 July 2022, 8scribo is a platform for writing and minting haiku on Tezos. After connecting a wallet, writers can compose a haiku and publish it as an NFT free of charge. The poem itself is stored on-chain; an image or video can be attached as an additional asset. The platform remains active, with 892 haiku written and published on-chain by July 2026.

### Summer Anthology 2022

From 7 to 9 September 2022, 8scribo collaborated with theVERSEverse and the crypto-poetry community on an open on-chain haiku festival. Writers were invited to mint reflections on the end of summer through 8scribo and tag their contributions with `#VVanthology22` for theVERSEverse’s *Summer Anthology 2022*.
