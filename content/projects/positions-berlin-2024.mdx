For Positions Berlin Art Fair 2024, ratata curated five digital works by Anna Malina, Ivona Tau, Kirk Sora, Salawaki and Theo Horsmeier. The presentation ran from 12 to 15 September in Hangar 6 at Flughafen Tempelhof during Berlin Art Week.

ratata developed the selection and realised the presentation. Galerie Greulich joined as a partner, while Upframe provided the digital displays used at the stand.

Alongside the exhibited works, visitors could scan a QR code and mint a free edition of Theo Horsmeier’s *Ontplitsing* on Tezos. The process supported both existing wallets and social sign-in, offering a direct route from viewing the work to receiving the digital edition.
