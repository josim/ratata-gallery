For Positions Berlin Art Fair 2024, ratata presented eight digital works by Anna Malina, Ivona Tau, Kirk Sora, Oli Sorenson, Salawaki, Tamiko Thiel and Theo Horsmeier. Oli Sorenson’s *Supply Chain / Chaîne logistique* and *DEFORESTATION* were curated by Art Mûr Gallery; Tamiko Thiel’s *Anthropocene Daze #4-9463* was curated by Gallery Yvonne Hohner. The presentation ran from 12 to 15 September in Hangar 6 at Flughafen Tempelhof during Berlin Art Week.

ratata developed the selection and realised the presentation. Galerie Greulich joined as a partner, while Upframe provided the digital displays used at the stand.

Alongside the exhibited works, visitors could scan a QR code and mint a free edition of Theo Horsmeier’s *Ontplitsing* on Tezos. The process supported both existing wallets and social sign-in, offering a direct route from viewing the work to receiving the digital edition.
