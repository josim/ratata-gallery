The Deutsche Bank AI & Art Night brought an exhibition and artist presentation to Rundgænger Gallery in Frankfurt on 4 May 2023. Organised by AI Hub Frankfurt in partnership with Deutsche Bank and Google Germany, the invitation-only event ran from 6 pm to midnight.

Adi Phil Coufal of ratata and Vladimir Alexeev (Merzmensch) presented a selection of their works and discussed the processes behind making art with AI. The presentation included Merzmensch’s short film *Hypnagogia*.
