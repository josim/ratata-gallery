# Overall Impression

The two-track identity is real and rigorously executed — this is the strongest visual system the site has had, and the evidence wiring (stats computed from the archive, not typed in) is exactly the "evidence over claims" principle made concrete. What undermines it is not the design system but **credibility cracks**: a fake "live" social feed at scroll position #2, dead press-download rows with invented file sizes, and non-interactive elements dressed as interactive. For an audience of grant reviewers, those cracks cost more than any aesthetic choice. The single biggest opportunity: make everything on the page either real or absent.

# What's Working

1. **Evidence wiring.** Stats, counts, and production metadata are computed from the archive (`page.tsx:96-104`, `62-69`) — "5 / 13 Projekte", "Valencia 2023". Most sites fake this; here it's structural.
2. **System discipline.** Zero radius, exactly one shadow, exactly two animations (both reduced-motion aware, `globals.css:89-94`), and `spur-*` tokens fully scoped so they cannot leak into catalogue pages. The inversion is carried down to per-track focus-ring colors.
3. **Accessibility groundwork above average:** `aria-pressed` lang toggle with `lang` attributes, `aria-hidden` marquee with an `sr-only` duplicate list, noscript reveal fallback, `scroll-mt` anchors.

# Priority Issues

- **[P0] SIGNAL ships fabricated social content.** `getFeed()` (`lib/feed.ts:114-116`) unconditionally returns `SAMPLE_FEED`, so the section always renders — verified in both DE and EN HTML, including six placeholder mentions ("Beispieltext — Erwähnung wird zur Laufzeit aus der API geladen") and German posts on the English page, under a blinking "live" dot.
  **Why it matters:** a Tezos Foundation reviewer hits an admission that the data is fake at the second scroll position. This fails the 60-second funder test outright and violates "complete at every state."
  **Fix:** gate the section on real data (`if (feed.sample) return null` in production or an env flag) — the hide-when-empty path already exists at `page.tsx:203`. Optionally also demote its position (see next).
  **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

- **[P1] Press rows are placeholder affordances with invented metadata.** `page.tsx:496-527` renders "Pressemappe (PDF) — 2,4 MB", "ZIP", "SVG" as rows styled identically to the linked contact row, but inert — and 2,4 MB is fiction for files that don't exist. PRODUCT.md's anti-references explicitly ban placeholder affordances.
  **Fix:** cut to the single "Presseanfragen" mailto row until assets exist; add rows only when files ship.
  **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

- **[P1] IA inverts "the archive is the product".** The social feed (sample or real) outranks Exhibitions in position and heading size (44px vs 38px). For funders, the exhibition record is the product; posts are garnish.
  **Fix:** move SIGNAL below the two-track section (or just above the footer); demote its heading one tier.
  **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout` (or fold into `/impeccable distill`)

- **[P1] Contrast failures (computed).** `spur-mut-soft` #9b978e = 2.67:1 on paper / 2.91:1 on card — used for press-row notes (`page.tsx:502`), signal timestamps at 10.5px (`page.tsx:248`), and the partners label (`page.tsx:536`). Everything else passes (mut 4.95, accent 4.75, dark-mut 5.45, dark-body 7.84, accent-dark 7.26).
  **Fix:** replace `spur-mut-soft` text uses with `spur-mut`; raise the inactive lang option to ~opacity-70.
  **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit` (verify), fixes via `/impeccable polish`

- **[P1] 104px hero likely wraps badly between 1100-1440px** *(inferred from source — no screenshot possible; verify in browser)*. `duo:text-[104px]` (`page.tsx:120`) hard-jumps from a 64px clamp cap at exactly the width where the hero column is narrowest (~540px at 1100px viewport).
